# Code of Conduct

We're here to build trustworthy agent memory, learn from each other, and enjoy the work.

## Keep it kind

- Be welcoming, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of view.
- Give credit, respect boundaries, and protect people's privacy.
- Keep credentials, private memory content, and customer data out of public discussions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized behavior, and repeated disruptive or promotional spam are not welcome.

## If something goes wrong

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close conversations, or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe and useful. We will explain moderation decisions when it is appropriate to do so.

Report concerns privately to `aniketkolte79@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled with care and kept as confidential as reasonably possible.

Thanks for helping Provena stay a calm place to build good software.
